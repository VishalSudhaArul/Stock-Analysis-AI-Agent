## 🚀 2. Recommended Feature Upgrades for AI Investment Desk

### 📈 Phase 1: Technical & Fundamental Intelligence Engine
1. **Interactive TradingView-Style Candlestick Charts**:
   - Upgrade basic price charts to interactive Candlestick charts supporting **1D, 1W, 1M, 1Y, 5Y** timeframes with volume bars.
   - Overlay technical indicators: **RSI (14)**, **MACD**, **EMA 50/200**, and **Bollinger Bands**.
2. **Comprehensive Fundamental Ratios & Peer Matrix**:
   - Add financial metric breakdown: Valuation (P/E, P/B, PEG), Efficiency (ROE, ROCE), Solvency (Debt/Equity), and Dividend Yield.
   - Peer Comparison table comparing the searched stock against top sector competitors (e.g., AAPL vs MSFT/GOOGL, TCS vs INFY/WIPRO).

### 🤖 Phase 2: AI Multi-Agent Advisory & Smart Scores (Angel One ARQ Style)
1. **AI Smart Composite Rating (0–100 Score)**:
   - Combine 4 sub-scores into an aggregate investment score:
     - **Valuation Score**: Is it undervalued or overvalued?
     - **Growth Score**: Revenue & Earnings Growth momentum.
     - **Financial Health Score**: Balance Sheet strength & Cash Flow stability.
     - **Sentiment Score**: News & Social Media Bullish/Bearish ratio.
2. **QGLP Institutional AI Framework (Motilal Oswal Style)**:
   - Provide an AI summary breakdown evaluating Quality, Growth, Longevity, and Price.

### 💼 Phase 3: Paper Trading & Portfolio Upgrades (Groww + Zerodha Style)
1. **SIP & Recurring Investment Simulator**:
   - Allow users to set up automated monthly paper SIPs ($500/mo into AAPL or INFY).
2. **Interactive Sector Heatmap**:
   - Visual treemap of portfolio holdings grouped by sector (Technology, Finance, Energy, Healthcare) with live color-coded P&L.
3. **Smart Stop-Loss & Target Price Alerts**:
   - Set browser or email notifications when a stock hits target price or stop-loss trigger.
